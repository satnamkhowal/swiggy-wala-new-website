Dry Season (May – September)

Weather: 12–20°C / 53–68°F
Highlights:
  • Sunny days with clear skies.
  • Lower humidity compared to other months.
  • Minimal rainfall.

Rainy Season (October – April)

Weather: 25–30°C / 77–86°F
Highlights:
  • Higher humidity levels.

  • Mix of sunshine and clouds.
Best for - Budget travelers, Fewer crowds.

Indonesia is best known for its stunning tropical islands, rich culture, beautiful beaches, active volcanoes, and world-famous destinations like Bali. It’s also popular for adventure activities, diverse wildlife, delicious local cuisine, and breathtaking natural landscapes.

For first-time travelers, Bali is usually the best place to visit first. It offers beautiful beaches, stunning temples, vibrant nightlife, rice terraces, waterfalls, and a wide range of activities for every type of traveler. Bali is also very tourist-friendly, making it easy to explore, stay, and enjoy comfortably.

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Many travelers can enter Indonesia with a visa-free entry, Visa on Arrival, or e-Visa for tourism. However, some countries must apply for a visa before traveling.

The best places to stay in Indonesia depend on the type of trip you want. For first-time visitors, Bali is the most popular choice because it offers luxury resorts, budget hotels, beach villas, and vibrant tourist areas.
