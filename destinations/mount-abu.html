Mount Abu · Suggested 2 days

A slower Rajasthan stop in the Aravalli hills.

Combine Nakki Lake, Dilwara Temples and one or two viewpoints without spending the whole weekend in traffic.

Suggested plan

Two days, paced for the hills.

Day 1 · Nakki Lake & town

Check in, explore Nakki Lake and the central market, then choose a permitted sunset point with enough time to return before roads become congested.

Day 2 · Dilwara & viewpoints

Visit the Dilwara Temples according to current visitor rules, then choose Guru Shikhar, Achalgarh or another nature stop based on time and weather.

Getting there

Keep road time realistic.

Mount Abu works well as a short break from Udaipur or as a slower stop in a broader Rajasthan route. Abu Road is the nearest major railway station, followed by a hill transfer.

Trip notes

Weekend demand

Hotels and roads can get busy on weekends and holidays, so refundable advance bookings can help.

Weather

Hill weather can change quickly. Keep extra travel time during rain, fog or peak traffic periods.

Responsible travel

Avoid littering, feeding wildlife and unsafe roadside stops; respect forest barriers and closing times.
