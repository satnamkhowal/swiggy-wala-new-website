Overview

Welcome to Grand Palace Resort & Spa, a luxury five-star retreat located in the heart of Dubai. Blending modern elegance with Arabian-inspired architecture, the resort offers travelers an unforgettable stay filled with comfort, relaxation, and world-class hospitality. Whether you are planning a romantic escape, family vacation, business trip, or luxury city getaway, Grand Palace Resort & Spa delivers a premium experience designed to make every moment exceptional.

From skyline-view suites and fine dining experiences to relaxing spa treatments and rooftop leisure spaces, every detail is carefully crafted for comfort and indulgence.

Guests can explore nearby attractions, cultural landmarks, shopping districts, and entertainment hubs—all within easy reach from the resort.

Questions & Answers

Check-in at Grand Palace Resort & Spa, Dubai begins from 3:00 PM, allowing our hospitality team enough time to fully prepare and sanitize each room to premium comfort standards before your arrival.

Standard check-out time is 12:00 PM, giving guests a relaxed morning to enjoy breakfast, spa facilities, or final moments at the resort before departure.

Breakfast depends on the room type and rate you select. Many of our rooms include a complimentary breakfast, while others offer it as an optional add-on. Please check the room details before booking or contact our support team for assistance.

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and throughout the hotel. Guests can enjoy reliable internet access for work, streaming, or staying connected during their stay at no additional cost.

Our rooms are thoughtfully equipped with modern amenities to ensure a comfortable stay, including complimentary Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a flat-screen TV, premium bedding, a private bathroom with toiletries, tea and coffee-making facilities, a minibar, a work desk, and daily housekeeping. Specific amenities may vary depending on the room type.

Many of our rooms feature a private balcony or terrace with beautiful views, while availability depends on the room category you choose. Please check the room details before booking or contact our team to confirm this feature for your selected room.

Have questions before booking? Our travel experts are available 24/7 to assist you.
